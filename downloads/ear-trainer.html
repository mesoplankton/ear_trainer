CHANGE AMOUNT, dB (for boost / cut / shelf)

Uncheck the amounts you don't want in a round — for example, leave only 3 dB for a harder mode. If none are checked, the app will ask you to pick at least one before starting a round.

In «Click on spectrum» mode only the frequency is checked: a click counts as correct if it lands within ±10% of the real frequency.

GRAPH (real time)

In frequency exercises it shows the real spectrum of whatever is currently playing; in the «Panorama» exercise — a left/right scale. After you answer, an exact marker for the correct value appears — so you can see where and how much the change was.

First choose the direction — boosted or cut — then click on the graph.
